Planet Plunder: Idle Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Planet Plunder: Idle (the "game") collects, why, and what choices you have. The game is made by the Planet Plunder team ("we", "us").

The short version

  • The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings and statistics are saved in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers rewarded ads that you choose to watch, for example to double a reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, such as your device's advertising IDAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location, derived from your IP addressAdvertising, fraud prevention
Interactions with ads and the gameAdvertising, analytics
Crash logs and diagnostic informationAnalytics, fraud prevention

This data is sent over an encrypted connection and is collected and processed by Google, not by us. You can read how Google uses this information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice later in the game's settings. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's Google settings.

Purchases

The game may offer an optional in-app purchase. Payments are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We receive only confirmation that a purchase was made so the game can unlock it. We do not receive your card or payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Deleting your data

We do not hold any of your data on our own systems. Your save file lives only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game. Data collected by Google for advertising is managed by Google; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings and manage your data through your Google account.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting different information, we will update this page and change the effective date above.
